AshenRelics 0.7.0-human-whispers

Changes:
- Revised Human quest chain wording to keep the early story focused on a prepared expedition, hidden funding, and guided paperwork.
- Renamed early reveal quest from The Name Vale to The Guarantor's Mark and changed Vale Guarantor Mark to Unmarked Guarantor Seal to avoid revealing House Vale too early.
- Renamed The Living Measure to The Surveyor's Glass.
- Renamed Continuation Ledger to Blackwater Payment Ledger.
- Added Broken Brass Meter as an evidence item discovered through Cargo Under Seal completion.
- Added Caelyn Vale relic whispers.

Whisper system:
- Runs only when a found relic has not whispered yet.
- Waits 10-20 minutes between eligible whispers.
- Each relic whispers once per saved character state.
- Skips while in combat, during intro playback, while dialogue is open, or while loot window is open.
- /ar whispers toggles whispers on/off.
- /ar whispertest immediately plays the next eligible whisper for testing.

Included whisper WAVs:
- manifest_whisper.wav
- lens_whisper.wav
- meter_whisper.wav
- ledger_whisper.wav
- contract_whisper.wav

Note:
- The Coin Without a King has whisper text ready, but no coin whisper WAV was provided in this patch.
